Going by their reported 2026 notices, SSC, the Railway Recruitment Boards and UPPRPB have all moved to **live photo capture**. Instead of asking you to choose a photo file, the form switches on your camera and takes the picture itself.

In one way that's a relief, since there's no KB limit or pixel size to fight with. The catch is that you get one go at it, often late at night in a dim room, and that photo follows you to the exam centre and to document verification. It's worth five minutes of preparation.

## Why forms switched to live capture

It's mainly about stopping impersonation. With live capture nobody can upload someone else's photo or an old, heavily edited one. It also means everyone's photo is taken in the same format. Later on, the captured photo is compared with your face at the centre and at verification.

## Set things up before you open the form

**Pick the better camera.** A phone's front camera is usually much better than a laptop webcam. If the portal works on mobile, use the phone. Wipe the lens with a soft cloth first. A smudged lens is the most common reason these photos come out hazy.

**Free up the camera.** Close video call apps and the camera app, and allow camera permission when the browser asks.

**Sort out the background.** Sit in front of a plain, light wall, white or off-white if you can. Move away from doors, posters, shelves and windows behind you. Keep about a metre between you and the wall so your shadow doesn't land on it.

**Get the light in front of you.** Face the window or the main light. Light from behind turns your face into a dark outline. A single bulb directly overhead puts shadows under your eyes and nose, so if the room is dim, put a lamp in front of you, slightly above eye level.

## While the camera is on

Look into the lens, not at your own face on the screen. Keep the camera at eye level (a stack of books under the phone works fine), mouth closed, neutral expression. Take off caps and dark glasses. Ordinary glasses often reflect light, so remove them unless the notice says otherwise. Keep your hair off your face so both ears show, and stay still until the capture finishes.

Most portals let you see the photo and retake it. Use that. Zoom into the preview if you can and check that your face is sharp and evenly lit before you accept it.

## After you submit

If the confirmation page or printout shows your photo, save it. Try not to change your look dramatically before the exam, because a very different appearance can slow down the identity check at the centre. Carry whatever documents the admit card lists, which usually includes a recent photo and a photo ID.

## The signature is still a file

Live capture only replaces the photo. You still upload a **signature** as a JPG, and the limits are tight: a reported 30 to 49 KB for RRB NTPC 2026, and 10 to 20 KB for SSC CGL. Make it ahead of time with the [signature resizer](/tools/signature-resizer), and see [how to scan your signature with a phone](/blog/how-to-scan-signature-for-online-form) if you haven't done it before.

## Before you start the form

- Lens wiped, camera permission allowed
- Plain light wall behind you, nothing else in the frame
- Light in front of you, not behind
- Camera at eye level, eyes on the lens
- No cap, dark glasses or filters
- Signature file ready and inside the KB range
