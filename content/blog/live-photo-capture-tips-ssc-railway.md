In 2026 several big recruitment bodies — including SSC, the Railway Recruitment Boards and UPPRPB, according to their reported notices — switched from uploaded photographs to **live photo capture**. Instead of choosing a file, the form opens your camera and takes the picture itself.

This removes a lot of KB and pixel confusion, but it brings a new problem: you get one chance at a good photo, often in a hurry, sitting in a dim room. This guide helps you get it right.

## Why forms moved to live capture

Live capture makes it harder to upload someone else's photo or an old edited one, which reduces impersonation. It also means the photo is taken in a standard format, so candidates do not have to resize it. The photo captured during the form is later compared with your face at the exam centre and during document verification.

## Before you start the form

### Choose the right device
- A **phone** usually has a better front camera than a laptop webcam. If the portal allows mobile applications, consider using your phone.
- Clean the camera lens with a soft cloth. A smudged lens is the most common cause of hazy photos.
- Close other apps that may be using the camera (video calls, camera apps).
- Allow camera permission in the browser when asked.

### Set up the background
- Sit or stand in front of a **plain, light-coloured wall**. White or off-white is ideal.
- Move away from doors, posters, shelves and windows behind you.
- Keep about a metre between you and the wall so your shadow does not fall on it.

### Get the light right
- **Face** the window or main light source. Light from behind turns your face into a silhouette.
- Avoid a single overhead bulb — it creates dark shadows under your eyes and nose.
- If the room is dark, place a lamp in front of you, slightly above eye level.

## During the capture

- Look straight into the camera, not at the screen.
- Keep the camera at eye level. Prop your phone on books if needed.
- Keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed.
- Remove caps, hats and dark glasses. Spectacles can cause glare; remove them unless the notice says otherwise.
- Make sure both ears are visible and your hair does not cover your face.
- Sit still until the capture is complete.

Most portals show a preview and let you retake the photo. Use it. Zoom in on the preview if you can and check that your face is sharp and evenly lit.

## After the capture

- Note whether the portal shows the photo on the confirmation page or printout. Save it.
- Keep your appearance broadly similar until the exam — a dramatically different look can slow down identity checks at the centre.
- Carry the documents your admit card asks for, usually including a recent photograph and a photo ID.

## You still need a signature

Live capture usually replaces only the photograph. The **signature is still uploaded** as a JPG, and the limits can be strict — for example a reported 30–49 KB for RRB NTPC 2026 and 10–20 KB for SSC CGL. Prepare it in advance with the [signature resizer](/tools/signature-resizer), and read [how to scan your signature with a phone](/blog/how-to-scan-signature-for-online-form).

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Lens cleaned, camera permission allowed
- [ ] Plain light wall behind you, nothing else in frame
- [ ] Light in front of you, not behind
- [ ] Camera at eye level, looking straight in
- [ ] No cap, dark glasses or filters
- [ ] Signature JPG ready and within the KB range
